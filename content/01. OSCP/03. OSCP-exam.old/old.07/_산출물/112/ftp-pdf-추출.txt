STAEDTLER 
a modern brand rich in tradition
STAEDTLER is one of the world’s leading manufacturers 
and suppliers of writing, colouring and drawing instruments 
as well as premium quality modelling/polymer clays. 
It is also one of the oldest manufacturing companies in Germany, 
drawing on centuries of experience and expertise in the 
development of innovative writing and drawing instruments.
It was in 1835 that Johann Sebastian STAEDTLER set up his 
pencil manufacturing plant in Nuremberg, and records show 
that one of the founder’s ancestors was actually making pencils 
as early as 1662. In 1853, the pencil maker from Nuremberg 
showcased his wares at the Exhibition of the Industry of All 
Nations in New York; just a few years later, these high-quality, 
German-made products had already made a name for themselves 
in France, Britain, Italy, Russia, America and the Middle East. 
Soon STAEDTLER was progressing to both round and hexagonal 
wood pencils in 48 colours, and by 1870 STAEDTLER employed 
54 people and already produced over 2 million pencils.
Today, STAEDTLER is an international company located in 
27 countries, employing over 2,100 people. It is Europe’s 
largest manufacturer of wood-cased pencils, offering a 
comprehensive range of stationery for school, home and 
work environments. Many of these products are now 
internationally recognised classics, such as the Noris HB 
pencil, the Mars plastic eraser and Lumocolor markers.
Throughout the company’s history, quality and innovation 
have remained the key to STAEDTLER’s continuing 
success, with regular breakthroughs in product design, 
performance and manufacturing process. Recent innovations 
include WOPEX, a groundbreaking way of producing 
pencils that maximises the usage of raw materials, and 
Noris colour, an enhanced range of coloured pencils 
made by the same innovative production process.

natural resources and the many people involved in the process.
All of our woodcased pencils (blacklead and colour) 
which are made in Germany are manufactured 
from PEFC or FSC® certified wood. 
PEFC provides a guarantee to purchasers of wood and 
paper products that they are promoting the sustainable 
management of forests. FSC® certification provides a credible 
link between responsible production and consumption of 
forest products, enabling consumers and businesses to 
make purchasing decisions that benefit people and the 
environment as well as providing ongoing business value.
